# Licensing Decision Required

SAFOR does not currently declare an open-source or commercial software license.

That is intentional: licensing is a product/ownership decision and must not be inferred by the runtime or an agent.

Before General Availability, the HUMAN must explicitly choose and publish the intended distribution terms, for example:
- proprietary/commercial,
- source-available,
- or a specific OSI-approved open-source license.

Until then, do not describe SAFOR as open source merely because its repository is public.
